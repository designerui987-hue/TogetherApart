How to Play Together

✨ Lumi (Light Weaver)

Lumi casts glowing starlight platforms in mid-air using Q. These platforms last 6 seconds and allow both players to cross wide chasms or gain vertical height.

⚡ Nova (Kinetic Shaper)

Nova emits kinetic energy pulses with Q to awaken ancient crystal mechanisms, activate moving platforms, and shift magnetic stones.
